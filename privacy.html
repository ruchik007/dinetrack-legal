Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

DineTrack POS is a restaurant point-of-sale. The restaurant owner is the controller of guest tickets and menu data. Contact privacy@dinetrack.app.

We process owner email and mobile, outlet name, hashed credentials, orders and payments you record (not card PAN), device pairing fingerprints, Play purchase tokens, and optional bug reports. We use this to run POS, sync, subscriptions, and (with consent) AdMob banners.

Processors include Supabase, Google Play Billing, and Google AdMob. We do not sell order or guest lists.

Delete your outlet in the app (More → Delete Outlet & Account) or via the account deletion page. EEA/UK users may request access, correction, deletion, or withdraw ad consent in the app.

The in-app Privacy Policy is the full version.
